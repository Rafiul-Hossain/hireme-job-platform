GET http://localhost:8000/api/admin/jobs?company_id=15

10. Filter by Status
GET http://localhost:8000/api/admin/jobs?status=inactive
This will show only inactive jobs.

11. Change Items Per Page
GET http://localhost:8000/api/admin/jobs?per_page=2
This will show 2 jobs per page.

12. Combined Filters
GET http://localhost:8000/api/admin/jobs?company_id=15&status=active&per_page=10
